### Opportunities Identified:
1. **Dominating the "9-Grid Instagram Campaign" Search Space:** With `campaign.html` and the deep educational content, the site is positioned to rank #1 for 9-grid Instagram strategy queries, a highly profitable, low-competition commercial niche.
2. **Leveraging Real Fortune 500 Endorsements for High E-E-A-T:** Real, verified account interactions from **Tic Tac India** and **7UP India** provide unparalleled trust signals that elevate authority for both Google and AI search engines.
3. **Structured Knowledge Graph (JSON-LD):** Linking Muhammed Sidhan (`Person`) to ZIDHANZID (`ProfessionalService`), his verified social profiles (Behance, Instagram, LinkedIn), and real case studies (Kibblix, FRAGRO, Bake Land).

### Technical Issues Identified & Resolved:
* **Missing `robots.txt`:** Generated and deployed to root with explicit directives for Googlebot, Bingbot, and modern AI crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot).
* **Missing `sitemap.xml`:** Generated and deployed to root with image sitemap extensions.
* **Missing Canonical Tags:** Formulated canonical tags for both `index.html` and `campaign.html` to eliminate duplicate content risks across worker staging URLs.
* **Missing Social Meta Tags:** Formulated full Open Graph and Twitter Card tags with high-impact preview imagery.
* **Missing Schema:** Built a comprehensive `@graph` JSON-LD schema with `WebSite`, `Person`, `ProfessionalService`, `FAQPage`, and `BreadcrumbList`.
