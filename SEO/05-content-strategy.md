## 2. Production-Ready FAQ Content (Ready for Implementation)

Embedding an FAQ section on the website resolves search intent for question-based queries and qualifies for Google FAQPage Schema.

### Question 1: What does a complete brand identity package include?
> **Answer:** A complete brand identity package by Muhammed Sidhan includes primary and secondary logo marks, icon marks, full typography hierarchy, curated color palette with color codes (HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone), comprehensive brand guidelines manual, social media kit, and print-ready collateral (business cards, letterhead, brochures).

### Question 2: What is a 9-Grid Instagram Campaign and how does it help my business?
> **Answer:** A 9-grid Instagram campaign is a strategic visual overhaul of the top nine posts on an Instagram profile, designed to function as an interactive digital brochure. Instead of disjointed individual posts, the unified grid clearly communicates your brand identity, core products, unique value proposition, testimonials, and contact details from the moment a potential client visits your page.

### Question 3: How long does a typical brand identity or design project take?
> **Answer:** Comprehensive brand identity projects typically take 2 to 4 weeks depending on the scope of research, concept development, and revisions. A dedicated 9-grid Instagram campaign or logo design typically takes 5 to 10 business days.

### Question 4: Do you work with international clients outside of India?
> **Answer:** Yes. Muhammed Sidhan works with founders, agencies, and businesses globally, with completed client projects across India, Qatar (Global Solution Catering), Indonesia (Zidhaf Kitchen), and Nigeria (MBG Integrated Farms). Seamless communication is managed via WhatsApp, Google Meet, and email.

### Question 5: What files and formats will I receive upon project delivery?
> **Answer:** You receive complete industry-standard production files including editable vector masters (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) for professional printing, and web-optimized raster formats (PNG with transparency, WebP, high-resolution JPEG) with full intellectual property ownership.
